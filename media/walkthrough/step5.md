### Local Ollama 3B and Audio Customization

Take the Manglish roasting to a personal level:

- **Local Ollama 3B**: With `ollama run llama3.2:3b` running on your machine, Dusty generates contextual roasts directly attacking your specific function names, file names, and programming life choices.
- **Instant Fallback**: If Ollama is offline, Dusty instantly falls back to his built-in bank of Manglish roasts with zero lag.
- **Custom Sound Effects**: Place custom `.mp3` or `.wav` files (like `sweep.mp3`, `crashout.wav`, `gobble.wav`) in `media/sounds/` to replace his procedural sound effects. If missing, retro synthesized audio plays automatically.
