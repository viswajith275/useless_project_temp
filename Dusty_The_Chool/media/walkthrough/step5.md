### Local LLM (Ollama) and Audio Customization

Take the Kerala meme roasting to a personal level:

- **Local LLM (Ollama)**: With `ollama run llama3.2:3b` running on your machine (recommended model for maximum emotional damage!), Dusty generates contextual roasts in your selected language (English or Manglish) attacking your specific code and life choices.
- **Instant Fallback**: If Ollama is offline, Dusty instantly falls back to his built-in 3-tier bank of Kerala meme roasts with zero lag.
- **Custom Sound Effects**: Place custom `.mp3` or `.wav` files (like `sweep.mp3`, `crashout.wav`, `gobble.wav`) in `media/sounds/` to replace his procedural sound effects. If missing, retro synthesized audio plays automatically.
