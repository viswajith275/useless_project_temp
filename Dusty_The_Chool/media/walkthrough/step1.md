### Meet Dusty the Chool

Dusty is a delightfully useless desktop broom living in your secondary sidebar. Instead of helping you finish your sprint, Dusty judges your code diagnostics and delivers savage Kerala meme roasts in English and Manglish when you make syntax mistakes.

- **Status and Dustpan (Muram)**: Monitors how many syntax crumbs he has swept up.
- **Rage Meter**: Tracks how annoyed Dusty is with your code.
- **Brutal Kerala Meme Roasts**: Drops famous Kerala internet and cultural memes (*Aaraattu Annan, Vazha / banana tree, KSRTC Swift, KSEB power cuts, Moral policing uncles, Food vloggers*) in English or Manglish.
- **Instant Language Toggle**: Switch between English and Manglish with one click or `Cmd+Alt+D L` (`Ctrl+Alt+D L`).

Open the sidebar view to meet Dusty.
