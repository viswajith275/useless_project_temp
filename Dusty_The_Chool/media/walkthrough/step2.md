### Sweep Engine and Dustpan (Muram)

Dusty stays asleep until you start his sweeping engine.

- Click the **SWEEP** button in the sidebar or run `Dusty: Toggle Engine`.
- Check the status bar in the bottom right corner for real-time status.
- When Dusty sweeps 5 syntax errors, his dustpan (Muram) becomes full. He chokes until you click **CLEAN DUSTPAN** or hit `Cmd+Alt+U C` (`Ctrl+Alt+U C` on Windows/Linux). Don't spam clean dustpan when it's already empty—3+ clicks will trigger a full crashout!
