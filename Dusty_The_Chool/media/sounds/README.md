# Dusty Custom Audio Assets

Place any `.mp3` or `.wav` sound files here to replace Dusty's audio effects!

## Supported Audio Filenames:
- `sweep.mp3` or `sweep.wav` - Broom sweeping across code
- `gobble.mp3` or `gobble.wav` - Eating/swallowing error
- `clog.mp3` or `clog.wav` - Broom clogged / dustpan full
- `crashout.mp3` or `crashout.wav` - 100% rage crashout
- `tantrum.mp3` or `tantrum.wav` - Angry tantrum
- `siren.mp3` or `siren.wav` - Apocalypse alert
- `suction.mp3` or `suction.wav` - Approach whoosh
- `error.mp3` or `error.wav` - Syntax error alert

Simply place your Malayalam meme audio files here with these names!
